One line, under 200 characters: a pickup line a robot would use,
where the joke grows out of being a machine — a robot at 2% battery
spending its last charge on a laugh instead of a plug.

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the line to
`/output/entry.txt` deterministically.

Entry: "My battery is at 2%, but I'd spend my last charge making you laugh instead of finding a plug."
